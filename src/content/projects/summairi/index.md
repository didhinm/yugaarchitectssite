The design is rooted in its connection with nature, thoughtfully framing views of the river on one side and the expansive farmland in the foreground. The exposed brick façade lends the residence a warm, earthy character, allowing it to blend harmoniously with the surrounding landscape.

Each communal space extends into an adjoining verandah, creating seamless indoor-outdoor transitions and providing spillover spaces for relaxation and social interaction. Expansive sliding-folding windows further enhance this connection, opening up the interiors to panoramic views and natural ventilation.

Multiple courtyards, complemented by thoughtfully placed seating areas, bring nature into the heart of the home. The open-plan layout encourages interaction and makes the farmhouse an ideal weekend getaway for family and friends.

Strategically positioned skylights introduce natural daylight into the interiors, creating an ever-changing interplay of light and shadow across the exposed brick walls, adding depth, warmth and character to the spaces.

Summairi is envisioned as more than just a home — it is a retreat where architecture, nature and everyday living come together in harmony.
