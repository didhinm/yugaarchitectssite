Spread across four levels, the house is thoughtfully organised, with semi public spaces on the ground and third floors, while the first and second floors accommodate shared family living.

Balconies and terraces extend the interiors outdoors, offering opportunities for greenery, capturing coastal breezes, and framing views of the surrounding landscape. These transitional spaces enhance the connection between the home and its setting while improving natural ventilation and daylight.

The architectural elevation is defined by cantilevered balconies and projecting sunshades, lending the structure a distinctive character while responding to the tropical climate. A restrained neutral palette, complemented by warm timber elements and exposed brick accents, brings depth and texture to the contemporary design.
